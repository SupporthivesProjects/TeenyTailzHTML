Frequently asked questions

What does a token buy?

Tokens are what a book is made with. A Bronze pack covers around two to three storybooks, Silver around six to seven. You spend them one book at a time.

How personal does it get?

Their name, their age, the characters beside them and the world it happens in. Tell us what they look like and the hero starts to look like them.

Can I change a story once it is written?

Yes. Go back through any of the four answers, change what you want, and write it again.

Where does the finished book live?

In your library, on your account. Open it on a phone, a tablet or a laptop and read it as many times as they ask.

Can more than one child use the same pack?

Yes. One pack, as many children as you like. Each book is written for whoever it names.

Do I need to be any good at writing?

No. You answer four questions in plain words. The writing is the part we do.
